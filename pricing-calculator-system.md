## Discount Guidelines

### When to Offer Discounts:
✅ **Nonprofit organizations:** 10-20% off
✅ **Referral from existing client:** 5-10% off
✅ **Multi-project commitment:** 10-15% off
✅ **Flexible timeline (3+ months):** 5-10% off
✅ **Cash payment upfront:** 5% off

### When NOT to Discount:
❌ "My budget is only X" (price shopping)
❌ "Exposure" or "portfolio piece"
❌ Rush projects (charge MORE, not less)
❌ Scope creep disguised as negotiation
❌ Clients who don't value your work

## Quote Template Checklist

### Initial Discovery Questions:
- [ ] How many pages do you need?
- [ ] Do you need e-commerce? How many products?
- [ ] Is your content ready (text, images)?
- [ ] What integrations do you need? (booking, CRM, payment, etc.)
- [ ] Do you have brand guidelines/existing design?
- [ ] What's your ideal timeline?
- [ ] Are you migrating from an existing site?
- [ ] Who is your target audience?
- [ ] What are 3 competitor sites you like?
- [ ] What's your budget range?

### Quote Document Should Include:
1. **Project Summary** - What you're building
2. **Scope of Work** - Detailed deliverables list
3. **Itemized Pricing** - Breakdown by feature/section
4. **Timeline** - Milestones with dates
5. **Payment Schedule** - 50% deposit, 25% midpoint, 25% on launch
6. **Assumptions** - What client provides, response time expectations
7. **Exclusions** - What's NOT included
8. **Revision Policy** - 2 rounds included, then hourly
9. **Terms** - Cancellation, refunds, ownership
10. **Next Steps** - How to accept and move forward

## Red Flags to Watch For

These should increase your price OR cause you to decline:

🚩 **Vague requirements** - "I'll know it when I see it" (+20% buffer)
🚩 **Many decision makers** - Slow approvals, conflicting feedback (+15%)
🚩 **Rush timeline** - Always charge premium (+25-50%)
🚩 **First website ever** - More education needed (+10-15%)
🚩 **"Simple" but actually complex** - Probe deeper, charge accurately
🚩 **Asking for discount before quote** - Price shopper, may be difficult
🚩 **Comparing you to offshore/cheap options** - Not your customer
🚩 **Wants ownership of everything** - Charge for source files (+$500-1,000)

## Profitability Check

### Target Metrics:
- **Effective Hourly Rate:** $100-150/hour minimum
- **Profit Margin:** 40-60% after expenses
- **Project Hours vs. Quote:** Track actual time to refine estimates

### Common Time Sinks to Account For:
- Client communication (emails, calls): 10-15% of project
- Revisions beyond scope: Charge hourly after 2 rounds
- Technical troubleshooting: Build in 10% buffer
- Project management: 5-10% of total time

### Calculate Your Minimum:
1. **Desired monthly income:** $________
2. **Billable hours per month:** ________ (typically 80-120)
3. **Minimum hourly rate:** Income ÷ Hours = $________
4. **Add 30% buffer:** Minimum × 1.3 = $________

Never go below your calculated minimum unless there's strategic value.

## Monthly Review Process

### Every Month:
1. **Review completed projects** - Did estimate match reality?
2. **Calculate effective hourly rate** - Total revenue ÷ actual hours
3. **Identify time sinks** - What took longer than expected?
4. **Update pricing** - Adjust based on demand and profitability
5. **Track client satisfaction** - Were they happy with price/value?

### Adjust Pricing When:
- Effective hourly rate < $100/hour (raise prices)
- Booked out 3+ months (raise prices 15-20%)
- Not enough leads (marketing issue, not price)
- Consistently underestimating (add 20% buffer)

## Final Tips

✅ **Price on value, not time** - What's it worth to the client?
✅ **Always build in buffer** - Things take longer than expected
✅ **Get 50% deposit** - Protects you and ensures commitment
✅ **Scope creep = change order** - Don't give away free work
✅ **Raise prices regularly** - 10-15% annually
✅ **Trust your gut** - If it feels too low, it is
✅ **Say no to bad fits** - Wrong client costs more than no client
✅ **Document everything** - Proposals, change requests, approvals
