Common questions about the process

Answers to common concerns that slow down decisions.

How involved do I need to be?

Enough to provide direction and feedback at a few key checkpoints. You won’t be asked to “manage the project,” but timely input keeps the timeline on track.

What if I miss a deadline or feedback window?

It happens. The timeline can shift if feedback or content comes late. The estimate stage clarifies responsibilities so expectations stay realistic.

What if my scope changes mid-project?

We’ll identify changes early and treat them as add-ons or a second phase. That keeps the original timeline and budget from ballooning unexpectedly.
