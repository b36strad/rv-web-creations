## 🚀 Quick Start

### 1. Customize Professional Information

Replace all bracketed placeholders:
- `[FIRM NAME]` - Your practice/firm name
- `[PROFESSION]` - Lawyer, Doctor, Accountant, etc.
- `[LOCATION]` - City, State
- `[YEAR]` - Year established
- `[XX]+` - Years of experience, clients served, etc.
- `[EMAIL]` - Professional email address
- `[PHONE]` - Office phone number
- `[Street Address]` - Physical office location

### 2. Add Professional Photos

**Required images:**
- Professional headshot (800x1000px) - Formal, trustworthy
- Office exterior or interior (1200x800px)
- Optional: Team photos if applicable

**Photography tips:**
- Professional attire
- Neutral, clean backgrounds
- High-quality, well-lit
- Conveys professionalism and approachability

### 3. Customize Service Areas

Update the six service area cards with your specific services:

```html
<!-- Example for Law Firm -->
<h3>Estate Planning</h3>
<p>Comprehensive estate planning including wills, trusts, and powers of attorney...</p>

<!-- Example for Dental Practice -->
<h3>General Dentistry</h3>
<p>Routine cleanings, exams, and preventive care for the whole family...</p>
```

### 4. Add Credentials

Fill in your actual credentials:
- **Education** - Degrees, universities, graduation years
- **Licenses** - State licenses, license numbers
- **Certifications** - Board certifications, specialized training
- **Memberships** - Professional associations
- **Awards** - Industry recognition

### 5. Compliance & Legal

**Important:** Customize legal/compliance language:
- Privacy policy link (create separate page)
- Professional privilege mentions (attorney-client, doctor-patient)
- Disclaimers appropriate to your profession
- Terms of service

**Consult with legal counsel** about required disclosures for your profession.

### 6. Setup Consultation Forms

The template includes two contact forms. Configure to:
- Send to your professional email
- Include required intake fields
- Add spam protection (reCAPTCHA recommended)
- Consider HIPAA-compliant form service if applicable

## 🎨 Customization

### Change Color Scheme

Edit `css/variables.css`:
```css
:root {
  --color-primary: #1e3a5f;      /* Navy blue - trustworthy */
  --color-primary-dark: #152942;  /* Darker navy */
  --color-accent: #c5a054;        /* Gold - professional */
}
```

**Recommended professional colors:**
- **Navy/Dark Blue** (#1e3a5f) - Trust, stability
- **Burgundy** (#800020) - Sophistication, law
- **Forest Green** (#228B22) - Growth, financial
- **Charcoal** (#36454F) - Professional, serious

### Adjust Typography

The template uses:
- **Body text**: Georgia (serif) - Traditional, readable
- **Headings**: Sans-serif - Modern, clear

To change fonts, edit `css/styles.css`:
```css
body {
  font-family: 'Georgia', 'Times New Roman', serif;
}
```

Sections are modular - remove or reorder as needed:
- About section

- Process (4 steps)
- Credentials

- Contact

## 🔒 Privacy & Compliance Notes

### HIPAA (Medical Professionals)
- Use HIPAA-compliant form services
- Don't collect PHI through regular forms
- Add HIPAA notice to contact forms
- Consider secure patient portal

### Attorney-Client Privilege
- Disclaimer: Submitting form doesn't create attorney-client relationship
- Note about confidentiality limits before representation
- Consider separate secure client portal

### Financial Services
- Required regulatory disclosures
- Investment advisor registration info
- Fee schedule transparency
- Fiduciary statement (if applicable)

**Always consult with compliance specialist for your profession.**
