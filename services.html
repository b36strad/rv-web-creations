Questions about your website build

Clear expectations on content, package scope, and post-launch updates.

“Do I need to have all my content ready first?”

No. If you have rough notes, existing pages, or a brochure, that’s enough to start. We’ll define what content is needed per page and what can be refined during the build.

“How do I choose between Starter and Growth?”

Starter covers a focused three-page site. Growth supports up to eight pages and adds foundational local SEO, eligible Google Business Profile setup or optimization, service area pages, and basic page-view analytics. See the Pricing page for full package details.

“What happens after the 30-day fix period?”

After the 30-day fix period, updates are scoped and quoted before work begins, so you only pay when you request changes. New features and larger changes are scoped separately; monthly maintenance subscriptions are not offered.
