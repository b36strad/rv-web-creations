Common questions before requesting an estimate

If any of these are on your mind, you’re not alone.

“Do I need to have all my content ready first?”

No. If you have rough notes, existing pages, or a brochure, that’s enough to start. We’ll define what content is needed per page and what can be refined during the build.

“What if I’m not sure what pages I need?”

That’s normal. The estimate process includes a quick discovery to determine page priorities based on your services, audience, and the action you want visitors to take.

“Will I be able to update the site later?”

Yes. The goal is a maintainable site structure. If you want, Website Care is included after launch (updates, backups, security, and small edits)..
