# Get Different Marketing Evaluation: RV Web Creations

**Date:** December 31, 2025

## Evaluation Criteria (from "Get Different" by Mike Michalowicz)
- **Differentiate:** Does the site stand out from competitors?
- **Attract:** Is the message bold and attention-grabbing?
- **Direct:** Is there a clear, compelling call to action?

---

## Page-by-Page Findings

### 1. Home (index.html)
- **Differentiate:**
  - Professional, clean, and modern design using Bootstrap.
  - Brand colors and logo are consistent, but the value proposition is similar to many web agencies.
- **Attract:**
  - Headline is clear but not especially bold or unique.
  - Visuals and layout are appealing, but messaging could be more memorable.
- **Direct:**
  - "Get a Quote" button is prominent and repeated for mobile users.

**Recommendation:**
- Add a unique, bold statement or visual that instantly sets you apart.
- Consider a more creative or unexpected headline.

## Overall Recommendations

- Highlight what truly makes you different—avoid generic claims.
- Make CTAs even more compelling with creative copy or offers.
