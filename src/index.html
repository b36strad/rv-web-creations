Design philosophy

Design is how your website works, not just how it looks.

The internet is full of gorgeous websites that win design awards but lose customers. I take a different approach: I build websites the way companies like Google, YouTube, and Amazon think about design — clear, fast, and focused on the user's next step.

1

What guides every layout

Every section on your site has a job — to inform, build trust, or convert. That's why I always prioritize usability and clarity over decoration.

  • Purpose over aesthetics: every section exists for a reason.
  • User experience first: visitors quickly find what they came for.
  • Mobile-ready & responsive: layouts adapt smoothly to all screens.
  • Lean & fast: no bloated images or unnecessary animations.
2

What this means for your business

A "beautiful" site that doesn't bring in leads or sales is a beautiful problem. I focus on making your website:

  • Understandable in a few seconds.
  • Action-oriented with clear buttons and paths.
  • Comfortable to use so people stay longer and do more.
Bottom line: design should make it easier for visitors to do what they came to do.
Process

A simple, three-step process to launch your website.

A clear process keeps your project moving and your stress low. I move from understanding your business to designing for real users, then building and refining a fast, responsive site.

1

Discover & Define

Goal: Understand your business, users, and what success looks like.

I start by getting clear on your goals and how your website should support them.

  • Clarify your primary website goal (leads, bookings, sales, subscriptions).
  • Identify your key user types and what they're trying to accomplish.
  • Map out essential pages and the main call-to-action on each.
Output: a simple, written plan for structure, content, and calls-to-action.
2

Design for Real Users

Goal: Create clean, scannable, responsive layouts with content leading the way.

I design around real people and real devices, not just screenshots on large monitors.

  • Row-and-column layouts that adapt gracefully to all screen sizes.
  • Consistent spacing using a simple 4-pixel-based system for visual rhythm.
  • Clear typography hierarchy for effortless reading.
Output: interactive mockups you can review and comment on.
3

Build, Launch & Improve

Goal: Ship a fast, reliable website and refine it based on real feedback.

I turn the design into a working website, test it, and then keep improving.

  • Clean, standards-based code optimized for speed and accessibility.
  • Testing on key devices and browsers for a reliable experience.
  • Post-launch tweaks based on user behavior and your feedback.
Output: a live, user-friendly website with a plan for ongoing improvements.

Foundation

The five pillars behind every website I build.

Every project is built around a small set of principles that keep your website user-friendly, focused, and effective — no matter your industry.

1

Content

People come to your site to accomplish something. I design around that purpose first.

  • Clear messaging that explains who you are, what you do, and why it matters.
  • Logical content flow that answers the right questions in the right order.
  • Calls-to-action that feel natural, not pushy.
Focus: goal-oriented copy and trust-building sections.
2

Layout

A simple, predictable layout keeps visitors comfortable and focused on what matters.

  • Row/column grid for consistent alignment and easy scanning.
  • Consistent spacing based on multiples of four for balance.
  • Clean, minimal navigation with obvious paths forward.
Focus: grid-based layout and mobile-first structure.
3

Typography

If your text is hard to read, nothing else matters. I keep typography clean and simple.

  • Typically one primary font used across the site for consistency.
  • Headings sized for quick scanning, body text sized for comfortable reading.
  • Contrast that passes accessibility guidelines.
Focus: readable on any device with a clear hierarchy.
4

Visuals

Visuals support the message — they don't distract from it.

  • Limited palette: usually black/white/gray with a single accent color.
  • Buttons and links use the accent color so actions are instantly recognizable.
  • Images and icons are crisp, optimized, and connected to your content.
Focus: brand-aligned, optimized visuals that feel intentional.
5

Performance

Your site should feel fast and natural on any screen — especially phones.

  • Layouts that gracefully reflow from desktop to tablet to mobile.
  • Lean code and optimized media to reduce load times.
  • Built with real-world usage in mind, not only large monitors.
Focus: mobile-first mindset and speed-conscious build.
Your website should keep working for you after launch. I offer ongoing support and updates, so your site can adapt as your business and your users’ needs evolve.
