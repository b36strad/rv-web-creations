What is Moxie?

Moxie is a secure, all-in-one client portal and project management platform designed for creative businesses. It streamlines every step of your project—from onboarding and contracts to communication, file sharing, invoicing, and support—so you always have clarity and control.

  • Track your project’s progress in real time
  • Access files, messages, and deliverables in one place
  • Sign contracts and pay invoices securely online
  • Submit support requests and get timely updates
With Moxie, you get the benefits of enterprise-level systems—without the hassle or confusion.

What Goes Into Your Website—Behind the Scenes

Delivering a professional website is more than just design and code. Here’s how I use industry-leading tools and a proven process to ensure your project is smooth, transparent, and successful from start to finish.

  • Personalized Onboarding: Every project starts with a discovery call and a custom proposal, managed through Moxie for clarity and convenience.
  • Clear Contracts & Milestones: Digital agreements, milestone tracking, and automated reminders keep your project on track and everyone accountable.
  • Collaborative Project Management: You get a client portal to view progress, share files, and communicate—all powered by Moxie’s secure platform.
  • Transparent Invoicing & Payments: Receive professional invoices, pay securely online, and track your payments and deliverables in one place.
  • Thorough Handover & Support: Final deliverables, documentation, and post-launch support are all organized and delivered through Moxie for a seamless experience.
  • Streamlined Support with Ticketing: After launch, you can submit support requests or questions through Moxie’s ticketing system, ensuring every issue is tracked and resolved efficiently.
Why it matters: My process is designed to save you time, reduce stress, and ensure you always know what’s happening with your project. You get the benefit of enterprise-level systems—without the hassle.
