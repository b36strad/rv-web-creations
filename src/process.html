Process

What to expect from first call to launch.

Step 1: Discovery & Fit

Initial conversation

No obligation

⏱️ Timeline: 30-45 minutes

  • • I talk about your business, goals, and your current website (if any).
  • • You'll get a plain-language overview of options and pricing ranges.
  • • If it's a good mutual fit, I move to a written proposal.

Step 2: Proposal & Agreement

Scope, price, and timeline

Everything in writing

⏱️ Timeline: 2-3 days

  • • You receive a summary of deliverables, timeline, and investment.
  • • I clarify any questions or changes before you approve.
  • • Once approved and initial payment is made, I reserve your slot.

Step 3: Content & Structure

What goes where

Collaboration

⏱️ Timeline: 1-2 weeks

  • • I outline your pages and key sections for each page.
  • • You provide text, photos, and any existing branding (or I simplify as needed).
  • • I finalize a simple sitemap before design begins.

Step 4: Design, Build & Launch

Development and handoff

Go live

⏱️ Timeline: 2-4 weeks

  • • Initial layout/wireframe for review.
  • • Full build with opportunities for feedback.
  • • Final testing, launch, and walkthrough of how to request changes or updates.
