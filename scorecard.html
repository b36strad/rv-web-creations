Intent & Relevance

If the right person lands here from search, do they immediately feel this page is for them?

Clear audience & problem in first screen
Within ~5 seconds, it's obvious who it's for and what problem you solve.
Headline matches likely search intent
Headline aligns with what people searched (not just brand language).
Immediate confirmation of relevance
Subhead/supporting copy quickly reinforces "I'm in the right place."
Pain points are explicitly named
The page clearly names the visitor's problem(s) in plain language.
Outcome is described as a transformation
It describes the before/after outcome so visitors can picture success.
Language matches the buyer
Copy uses customer language (not internal jargon or overly technical wording).

Value Proposition Clarity

Can a first-time visitor understand what you do, who it's for, and the outcome within 5 seconds?

One clear primary service or outcome
A single main pricing or outcome is emphasized (no competing priorities).
Supporting sections reinforce the main idea
Sections consistently support the main promise instead of branching into unrelated claims.
Headings communicate meaning (not fluff)
Headings convey benefits and clarity—not vague slogans.
Problem + result are clear (before/after)
The problem and result are concrete, not abstract.
Differentiation is stated
The page explains why this option is different/better than alternatives.

Trust & Credibility

Do you provide enough proof and legitimacy signals for skeptical visitors to believe you?

Proof placed near decision points
Testimonials/reviews/case proof appear where visitors decide.
Real outcomes or examples shown
Specific results, examples, or process proof (not generic claims).
Business legitimacy is obvious
Clear business info, credentials, and risk reducers are easy to find.
Primary service emphasized clearly
The main service/outcome is reinforced multiple times in plain language.
Supporting pages logically connected
Navigation and internal links reinforce the main focus.
No buried or de-emphasized priorities
Key pages/pricing options aren't hidden behind vague labels or extra steps.
Testimonials are present and specific
Testimonials include specifics (context + outcome), not just praise.
Clear process or steps are explained
A simple process/steps section reduces uncertainty and builds trust.

Conversion Path Clarity

Is the next step obvious, specific, and low-friction?

One dominant primary CTA
A single main CTA stands out throughout key sections.
CTA matches visitor readiness
CTA fits first-time visitors (e.g., 'Get a quote' vs 'Book now' depending on context).
No competing or confusing actions
Secondary CTAs don't distract from the main action.
CTA wording is benefit-driven
CTA text is specific and benefit-driven (not generic "Contact").
CTA repeats throughout the page
The primary CTA is repeated at logical points so scanners can act.
Next-step expectations are clear
The page explains what happens after clicking (timing, steps, what you get).

Structure & Scannability

Can visitors scan the page and still understand it quickly?

Logical, scannable section order
The page follows a natural sequence: promise → proof → details → next step.
Clear separation of ideas
Each section has a purpose; content is not visually or conceptually jumbled.
Important info prioritized
Key benefits and proof appear before less important content.
Above-the-fold is clear and persuasive
Above the fold confirms relevance + provides a clear next step.
Key info is easy to find
Key info (pricing, proof, next step) is easy to find without excessive scrolling.

Friction, Objections & UX

Do you reduce anxiety, answer objections, and keep the mobile experience frictionless?

Message clarity without zooming
Headline, subhead, and pricing are readable and clear immediately.
Buttons and links easy to tap
Tap targets are comfortable; no accidental clicks.
No critical content hidden
Key content isn't buried in accordions or missing on mobile.
Objections are acknowledged
Common objections (cost, fit, timing, risk) are addressed proactively.
Clear expectations are set
Expectations are set (timeline, steps, what you need from the client).
Risk reversal exists
Risk is reduced (no-pressure language, transparency, guarantees where appropriate).
FAQs or reassurance section exists
An FAQ/reassurance section answers common questions quickly.
Readable typography (mobile)
Typography is readable on mobile (size, contrast, line-height).
Layout is clean and not overwhelming
Layout feels calm and uncluttered; sections are easy to parse.
Contact options are easy to find
Contact options are easy to find (footer/header, consistent placement).
