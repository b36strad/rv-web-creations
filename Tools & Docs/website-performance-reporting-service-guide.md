# Website Performance Reporting Service Guide

**Status:** Internal planning reference. Do not publish or sell this service until the readiness checks below are complete.

## Purpose

This guide outlines an optional, plain-English reporting service for local service-business clients. The goal is to help owners understand what is happening on their website and identify a few sensible next steps, not to overwhelm them with dashboards or promise rankings, leads, or business results.

The report is a separate service from a website build. It does not create a monthly maintenance obligation and does not include making website or SEO changes.

## 1. Website Performance Snapshot

**Price:** $149 one time  
**Timing:** First review after the site has been live for 60-90 days and tracking has been checked.  
**Format:** A concise, approximately 1-page written report delivered by email. No meeting is included.

### Included in the report

1. **Reporting period and data notes**
   - Exact start and end dates covered.
   - Whether the report uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), Google Search Console, or both.
   - Any gaps, recent tracking changes, low data volume, or other limitations that affect interpretation.

2. **Website visits**
   - GA4 sessions and users for the reporting period, when available and configured consistently.
   - Main traffic channels or sources, such as organic search, direct, or referral.
   - A brief explanation of what those terms mean in this report.

3. **Pages visitors viewed**
   - The most-viewed pages and, where useful, the main landing pages.
   - One or two observations about whether visitors are reaching useful service or contact information.

4. **Search visibility, if Search Console is connected**
   - Search clicks and impressions for the period.
   - A small selection of relevant search queries or pages, if the data is available.
   - A plain-language explanation that impressions and average position are search indicators, not a guarantee of visits or leads.

5. **Tracked contact actions, only if verified**
   - Counts for configured form-submission events and phone-link clicks, shown separately.
   - A clear note that a phone-link click does not confirm that a call connected, and a form event does not necessarily confirm a qualified lead.
   - If events are not configured and tested, state that inquiry activity cannot be measured reliably; do not infer or estimate it from page views.

6. **Takeaways and next steps**
   - Up to three concise observations.
   - Two or three prioritized recommendations, labeled as suggestions rather than guaranteed outcomes.

### Not included

- A review call, implementation of recommendations, ongoing monitoring, tracking repairs, conversion-event setup, call-tracking service, SEO campaign, content writing, or ranking/lead guarantees.

## 2. Quarterly Website Check-In

**Price:** $225 per review  
**Frequency:** One review every three months; charge per review, with no monthly subscription required.  
**Format:** A 1-2 page written report plus a 20-30 minute remote review call.

### Included in the report

1. **Period-over-period comparison**
   - The latest 90-day period compared with the previous 90 days, when tracking was consistent across both periods.
   - State whether counts or percentage changes are being shown. Avoid emphasizing percentage changes when the starting count is very small.
   - Do not include year-over-year comparisons until at least 12 months of comparable data exists.

2. **Traffic overview**
   - GA4 sessions and users, primary channels/sources, and notable changes.
   - A short explanation of which sources appear to be bringing visitors to the site.

3. **Page performance**
   - Top pages and landing pages for the period.
   - Meaningful changes in page activity, with context where available (for example, a seasonal service or a recent site update).

4. **Search overview, if Search Console is connected**
   - Search clicks and impressions, notable queries, and pages appearing in search.
   - Separate Search Console search data from GA4 visit data; the metrics use different definitions and should not be combined as if they were the same count.

5. **Contact actions, only if verified**
   - Separate counts for configured form submissions and phone-link clicks.
   - Include the same qualification that tracked actions do not prove a qualified lead or completed phone call.
   - Note any known event-tracking gaps or changes during the period.

6. **Prioritized recommendations and discussion**
   - Up to three recommendations, ordered by likely usefulness and effort.
   - A 20-30 minute call to explain the report, answer questions, and agree on which recommendation (if any) the client wants scoped as separate work.
   - A brief written recap of agreed next steps after the call.

### Not included

- Making the recommended changes, monthly monitoring, technical or SEO audits, new content, ad management, CRM configuration, call-tracking subscriptions, or guaranteed outcomes. Quote any implementation or expanded analysis separately before beginning.

## Frequency Guidance

- **First report:** Wait 60-90 days after launch or after reliable tracking begins. This provides a more useful initial sample than reporting immediately after launch.
- **Default ongoing frequency:** Quarterly. For many small local businesses, a 90-day interval is a better balance between useful trend data and the effort of reviewing it.
- **Monthly reporting:** Do not offer monthly reporting as the default at launch. Consider it later only when a client has enough activity and the service also includes meaningful ongoing work or decisions each month.
- **Low traffic or sparse data:** Extend the reporting period, report the small counts plainly, or recommend waiting for more data. Do not manufacture conclusions to fill a report.

## Requirements Before Accepting a Report

- The client owns their Google accounts and analytics property. Request the minimum access needed, preferably read-only access for reviewing reports.
- Confirm which reporting tools are available. GA4 is needed for website activity; Google Search Console is needed for Google organic search performance.
- Confirm the property and site are collecting data for the correct domain and reporting period.
- Check that tracking was active for the full period and identify known interruptions, domain changes, consent changes, or duplicate measurement that could make comparisons unreliable.
- Verify each event before presenting it as a measured contact action. A page view is not a lead measurement.
- Ask the client for brief context about relevant changes, promotions, seasonal patterns, and whether inquiries felt useful. Do not request or put personal information from individual leads into the analytics report.
- Do not send personally identifiable information to GA4 or include individual visitor details in a client report. Confirm the client is responsible for appropriate privacy and consent notices; legal advice and consent-management implementation are outside this service unless separately agreed.

## Measurement and Language Rules

- Use plain language and define any technical term that cannot be avoided.
- Identify the source of every metric. Do not present GA4 sessions, Search Console clicks, form events, and phone-link clicks as interchangeable.
- Distinguish measured facts from interpretation and recommendations.
- Say “phone-link clicks,” not “calls,” unless a separate call-tracking system verifies actual calls.
- Say “tracked form submissions,” not “leads,” unless lead quality and duplicates are independently confirmed.
- Do not claim that SEO caused a change from a simple before-and-after comparison. Traffic can change for many reasons, including seasonality, promotions, market demand, and tracking changes.
- Do not promise search rankings, traffic growth, inquiries, revenue, or a particular business result.
- If the tracking is incomplete or questionable, explain the limitation and offer a separately scoped tracking review instead of presenting unreliable numbers as facts.

## Scope and Billing Boundaries

- The existing Growth website package includes basic GA4 setup and page-view tracking. That alone does not include a report or verified lead tracking.
- Event setup, tracking troubleshooting, and call-tracking tools are separate work and should be scoped and quoted before reporting if they are needed.
- Reports cover the agreed property, domains, period, metrics, and deliverables only. Additional sites, custom analysis, extra meetings, or revised reports require a separate quote.
- The Snapshot is a one-time service. The Quarterly Check-In is optional and billed per review; clients can skip a quarter or stop requesting reports without affecting their website.
- A report call is for explaining the report and discussing next steps, not an open-ended consulting session.

## Suggested Client-Facing Positioning

When ready to publish, describe this as an optional **Website Performance Review**: a concise, plain-English explanation of website activity, search visibility when Search Console is available, verified tracked contact actions when configured, and practical next steps. State the reporting frequency, price, and exclusions clearly. Do not describe it as ongoing maintenance or imply that every client will have enough data for firm conclusions.
