## Dropped / De-duplicated Items

Removed because they overlapped with higher-resolution criteria already present, or fit better elsewhere.

- `value_chk1` — Headline states outcome (not vague slogan)
- `value_chk2` — Who it’s for is obvious (industry / type of buyer)
- `trust_chk2` — Examples / work samples are visible or one click away
- `trust_chk4` — Real-world signals: photos, logos, credentials, years, reviews
- `messaging_chk4` — Scannable formatting (short sections, bullets, clear headings)
- `cta_chk1` — One primary CTA is dominant
- `path_chk2` — Sections follow a persuasion order (not random)
- `path_chk4` — Visual hierarchy supports scanning (headlines, spacing, bullets)
- `ux_chk2` — Buttons/tap targets are easy on mobile
