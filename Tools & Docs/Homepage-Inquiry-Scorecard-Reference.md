
# Homepage Inquiry Scorecard – Consolidated Reference

This document consolidates the full conversation into a single, structured reference.
It is intended to serve as a **foundational guide** for positioning, tooling, and service design
around homepage inquiry optimization.

---

## 1. Core Insight

Most businesses with websites **do not have a traffic problem**.  
They have a **conversion problem**.

Visitors are already arriving via SEO, ads, referrals, or social media, but:
- They do not clearly understand the offer
- They do not trust the business enough
- They do not feel safe taking the next step

The homepage is the **make-or-break layer** where interest turns into inquiries.

---

## 2. Positioning Decision

### Specialist vs Swiss-Army-Knife

**Strategic choice:**  
Position as a **homepage inquiry conversion specialist**, not a general web agency.

Rationale:
- Businesses prefer specialists for high-impact problems
- Conversion is the bottleneck that unlocks all other marketing
- A focused message is more credible than “we do everything”

Positioning statement (example):

> “I help businesses that already have traffic turn their homepage into a reliable inquiry engine.”

---

## 3. Homepage as a Landing Page

The homepage should function like a **high-level landing page**, not a brochure.

That means:
- Clear outcome-driven headline
- Single primary CTA
- Structured persuasion flow:
  1. Clarity
  2. Trust
  3. Proof
  4. Risk reduction
  5. Action

A brochure-style homepage **can still convert** if:
- The CTA is clear
- The message is customer-centered
- Objections are handled before asking for contact

---

## 4. Why Homepage-Only Focus Works

Reasons to focus on homepage only:
- It carries the highest decision-making burden
- Most visitors never reach inner pages
- If the homepage fails, nothing else matters
- It keeps audits defensible and repeatable

Whole-site audits:
- Add ambiguity
- Increase scope creep
- Reduce clarity of recommendations

Homepage-only audits:
- Are easier to explain
- Are easier to sell
- Produce faster results

---

## 5. The Homepage Inquiry Scorecard

### Purpose

A diagnostic framework to:
- Objectively evaluate why a homepage does not generate inquiries
- Remove bias from self-evaluation
- Prioritize fixes by impact

---

## 6. Core Categories (7)

1. Value Proposition Clarity  
2. Trust & Proof  
3. Customer-Focused Messaging  
4. Call-to-Action Strength  
5. Conversion Path & Structure  
6. Objections & Risk Reduction  
7. UX & Mobile Usability  

These categories reflect **decision friction**, not design preferences.

---

## 7. Weighting Logic

Higher weight = higher impact on inquiries.

Typical weighting:
- Value Proposition: High
- Trust & Proof: High
- CTA Strength: High
- Structure & UX: Supporting
- Technical items: Foundational, but not persuasive

Weights are based on:
- Conversion psychology
- Real-world buyer behavior
- Order of decision-making

---

## 8. Scoring Scale (0–5)

Each category and checklist item uses the same rubric:

- **0** – Missing entirely
- **1** – Present but ineffective
- **2** – Below average, major gaps
- **3** – Acceptable baseline
- **4** – Strong, effective
- **5** – Best-in-class

This creates consistency and removes guesswork.

---

## 9. Manual vs Automated Evaluation

### Manual (Recommended Starting Point)
- Expert-controlled
- Objective
- High trust
- Best for consultations and audits

### Fully Automated (Future Vision)
- Impressive, but complex
- Requires heuristics + AI
- Limited accuracy for trust, clarity, and persuasion
- Best as a lead magnet, not a final verdict

Hybrid approaches are possible later.

---

## 10. Customer Interaction vs Expert Control

Key insight:
> Business owners are biased about their own websites.

Risks of self-scoring:
- Overrating clarity
- Missing trust gaps
- Misjudging CTA effectiveness

Recommended model:
- Use the scorecard **internally**
- Present results to clients
- Walk them through findings
- Position yourself as the objective expert

Optional:
- Public “lite” version as a lead magnet
- Full audit remains expert-led

---

## 11. Lead Magnet Strategy

The scorecard works well as a lead magnet when:
- It diagnoses problems
- It explains consequences
- It shows *what* to fix, not *how* to build it

Best formats:
- Downloadable PDF
- Homepage-only audit framing
- Clear next-step CTA:
  “Want me to score your homepage and prioritize fixes?”

This attracts serious buyers, not price shoppers.

---

## 12. Homepage Redesign vs Full Site Redesign

Key clarification:
- Homepage is the highest ROI page
- Fixing it alone often improves inquiries significantly

Objection: “$X for one page?”
Reframe as:
- Revenue-impact optimization
- Conversion bottleneck removal
- Foundation for future site updates

Phased approach:
1. Homepage optimization
2. Align key inner pages later (optional)

---

## 13. Tooling Decisions

### No-Build Requirement

Best solution:
- **Web Components (Vanilla JS)**

Why:
- Drop-in anywhere
- No build process
- No framework dependency
- Long-term maintainability

React/Vue:
- Not required for components
- Better suited for full apps later
