# Homepage Evaluation

**Business:** Leading Edge Finishings, LLC  
**Page reviewed:** Homepage implemented in `src/pages/index.astro`  
**Assessment date:** October 11, 2026  
**Overall score:** 63/100  
**Criteria assessed:** 40 of 40

## Executive Summary

The homepage gives visitors several useful reasons to trust the business: it identifies the company as licensed and insured, names its Woodbury location, shows before-and-after project photography, and links to Facebook recommendations. Contact options are also easy to find, and the project-request form provides a clear way to share the details of a job.

The main opportunity is to make the first screen speak more directly to a homeowner’s search. The headline is the business name, while the supporting language describes a broad mix of home improvements and seasonal lighting without stating a specific customer problem or distinctive reason to choose this company. On mobile, the Facebook video embed is wider than the screen and causes horizontal page scrolling. Addressing the mobile overflow and sharpening the headline and offer would make the page clearer and easier to act on.

## What Is Working

- The page quickly identifies the company’s location and licensed/insured status.
- Real before-and-after project imagery gives visitors a concrete starting point for evaluating the work.
- Services, projects, company information, and contact routes are connected through clear navigation and homepage links.
- Visitors can choose among a quote/contact page, phone, or text rather than being forced into a single contact method.
- The contact page’s project-request form guides visitors through project type, location, budget, timing, and project details, and provides a submission confirmation.

## Recommended Priorities

1. **Fix the small-screen video overflow.** The holiday-lighting Facebook embed is set to 500px wide; at a 390px viewport, the document expands to 520px and can be scrolled sideways. Make the embed responsive to its container and verify the section at common mobile widths.
2. **Rewrite the hero headline and supporting line around the local customer and offer.** Keep the business name visible, but make the primary heading clarify the service and location (for example, a draft such as “Home improvements in Woodbury, NJ”). Confirm the final wording with the business before publication.
3. **Choose and emphasize the main service focus.** Decide whether broad interior/exterior improvements or a narrower set of specialties should lead. Keep seasonal lighting visible but subordinate if it is not a year-round priority.
4. **Add a concise project process and response expectation.** A short sequence such as inquiry, project discussion, and next step can reduce uncertainty. State a response timeframe only after confirming what the team can reliably meet.
5. **Bring specific review evidence closer to an inquiry point.** If permission and accurate source details are available, show one or two concise customer comments with context near the project gallery or contact action instead of relying only on an outbound reviews link and aggregate figure.

## Assessment Notes

- **Scoring method:** Each criterion is scored 0–2. Category score is `earned points / available points × 100`. The overall score is the equal-weighted average of the six category percentages. This is a transparent assessment convention; the scorecard itself does not define numeric weights.
- **Coverage:** 40 of 40 listed criteria were scored. The scorecard page displays “36 Criteria,” while its six categories contain 40 individual checks; all listed checks were included. No criteria were marked not verified or not applicable.
- **Page selection:** There is no root-level `index.html` in this workspace. `Tools & Docs/Website-Scorecard/index.html` is a launcher for the scorecard widget, not the business homepage. The evaluated homepage is the complete Astro route at `src/pages/index.astro`.
- **Review conditions:** The rendered homepage was inspected at 1440px desktop width and 390px mobile width. The mobile navigation was opened, and project images were checked after scrolling them into view. The Facebook embed depends on an external service; its fixed-width layout was visible during review.
- **Limitations:** This review does not measure search rankings, leads, analytics, page-speed metrics, formal accessibility conformance, or real-user behavior. No public production URL was provided, and the quote form was inspected but not submitted.
