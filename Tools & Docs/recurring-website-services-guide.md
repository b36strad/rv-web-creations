# Recurring Website Services Guide

**Status:** Internal planning reference. Pilot with a small number of clients before adding these services to the public website.

## Recommendation

The most practical first recurring offer for RV Web Creations is a small **Website Care & Updates** plan. It gives clients ongoing help with their website while keeping the commitment and monthly cost understandable. It is a better first monthly service than a report-only subscription, because clients receive a concrete service each month.

Do not assume most clients will subscribe. A small number of clients who have recurring needs can still create useful monthly revenue. At $99 per month, three clients would represent $297 in monthly gross revenue and five clients $495, before taxes, expenses, and delivery time.

## First Offer: Website Care & Small Updates

**Pilot price:** $99 per month  
**Billing:** Monthly, with the client able to cancel before the next billing period.  
**When it begins:** After the included 30-day post-launch fix period has ended. The included fix period remains part of the original website build and is not replaced by this plan.

### Included

- One small content-update request per month, limited to 30 minutes of work. Examples include updating business hours, revising existing service copy, replacing a supplied photo, or changing a promotion.
- A monthly site health check covering whether the website is reachable, whether its HTTPS certificate is working, and whether the contact form can successfully deliver a test submission.
- A brief check of the main contact links, such as click-to-call and email links.
- Administration of the client's hosting and domain settings as needed for the agreed website service, such as deployment and routine configuration.
- Email support for requests related to the website, with a stated response time of three business days.

### Not included

- Domain registration, hosting, email, or other third-party subscription charges.
- New pages, redesigns, new features, copywriting, SEO campaigns, analytics event configuration, or work beyond the monthly 30-minute update allowance.
- Emergency, same-day, or after-hours support.
- Guaranteed uptime, search rankings, website traffic, inquiries, or revenue.

Unused update time does not roll over. Larger requests should be estimated and approved separately before work begins. If the client does not need a monthly update or support, continue to offer individually scoped, one-time work instead of pressuring them into a subscription.

## Domain and Hosting Responsibilities

- The client owns the domain and hosting accounts and pays the providers directly.
- RV Web Creations administers the accounts and website configuration needed to deliver the agreed service, with the client's authorization.
- The monthly service fee is for RV Web Creations' labor and support. It does not include or reimburse third-party provider costs.
- Keep account ownership with the client and use delegated access where the provider supports it. Do not make the agency the sole owner of a client's domain or hosting account.
- Explain that provider outages, account restrictions, billing issues, or delays outside RV Web Creations' control may affect the website and are not covered by a promise of uninterrupted availability.

## How to Pilot the Offer

1. Ask a few past or current clients whether they expect regular website changes or would value having someone administer the site after launch.
2. Offer the plan to two or three clients whose needs fit the defined scope. Make clear that it is optional and separate from the website build.
3. Track time spent on health checks, client communication, account administration, and edits for each client.
4. After the first three months, compare actual work with the $99 monthly fee. Adjust the price, time allowance, response time, or included checks if the service takes longer than expected.
5. Keep the public website's existing language about separately paid domain and hosting costs. When the care plan is ready to publish, clarify that clients continue to pay providers directly while the monthly plan covers RV Web Creations' administration and care work.

## Later Option: Local Presence Support

Consider this only after building confidence with Google Business Profile and local search work, and after clients express interest in help beyond the website itself.

**Possible pilot range:** $199-$299 per month. This is a planning estimate, not a researched universal market rate. Set a firm scope and confirm the price against actual delivery time before offering it.

A narrowly scoped version could include:

- One client-approved Google Business Profile post per month, using information and approved images supplied by the client.
- One monthly profile-accuracy review, such as checking hours, services, links, and business details.
- Draft responses to a limited number of new reviews for client approval; the client remains responsible for approving and publishing responses unless account permissions and responsibilities are explicitly agreed.
- A short quarterly local-search summary, if the client has connected Google Search Console and enough data is available.

It should not include a promise of higher rankings, a particular number of leads, unlimited review responses, ongoing content production, citation cleanup, or broad SEO work unless those items are specifically scoped and priced. Do not offer this plan until you can deliver each recurring task consistently.

## Analytics Reports: Separate, Occasional Service

Analytics reporting is useful as an optional, separately billed service, but it is not the primary monthly recurring offer at this stage.

- **Website Performance Snapshot:** $149 one time, after 60-90 days of reliable data.
- **Quarterly Website Check-In:** $225 per review, including a short written report and a 20-30 minute review call.
- Report only metrics that are configured and verified. Basic GA4 page-view tracking does not by itself measure inquiries.
- See [website-performance-reporting-service-guide.md](website-performance-reporting-service-guide.md) for the report contents, measurement caveats, and readiness checklist.

These prices are starting pilot prices, not fixed industry standards. Review them after tracking delivery time and client response.

## Service Boundaries

- The current Starter and Growth website packages are one-time builds and include 30 days of post-launch fixes. The Growth package includes basic GA4 page-view tracking.
- A care plan is optional after the included fix period; it is not a prerequisite for owning or operating the website.
- Avoid selling generic maintenance tasks that do not apply to the actual site. For a custom static website, routine plugin updates may not exist; describe the real checks and support being provided instead.
- Keep hosting and domain charges separate from service fees, consistent with the current pricing page.
- State the included time, turnaround, exclusions, billing cadence, cancellation terms, and process for out-of-scope requests in writing before accepting payment.
