  2. For Website/Digital: Use the "Medium (Digital)" version

    • Windows: Windows+Shift+S, then drag around the logo
  4. Recommended sizes:
    • Business card: 600-800px wide PNG
    • Website header: 300-400px wide PNG
    • Social media: 400-600px wide PNG
