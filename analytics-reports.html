Common questions

Do I need to give up control of my analytics account?

No. You add me with Viewer access, which can be removed at any time. You stay the owner of your account and your data.

What do I need before signing up?

A live website with Google Analytics 4 installed and some history of data. If tracking isn’t set up yet, we’ll quote the setup first so your reports are accurate.

How is this different from the basic tracking in a Growth build?

Growth includes basic Google Analytics 4 page-view tracking. This add-on is the ongoing interpretation: what the numbers mean and what to change.

Will the report improve my rankings or leads?

Not by itself. A report shows what is happening and where the opportunities are. Results depend on acting on the recommendations, and no outcomes are guaranteed.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. You can start with a Snapshot and move to quarterly or monthly reports whenever it makes sense.
