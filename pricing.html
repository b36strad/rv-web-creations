Hosting and updates

Domain registration and hosting are separate third-party costs. I’ll recommend a provider and plan, share setup and renewal fees, and get your approval before you purchase directly from the provider.

After the included 30-day fix period, updates are scoped and quoted before work begins. You only pay when you request changes; no monthly maintenance subscription is required. Larger changes and new features are scoped separately.

Local SEO setup does not guarantee search rankings or lead volume. Paid ads, ongoing content production, and CRM or funnel management are not included.

Questions before choosing a package

Clear answers about scope, timing, and what happens after launch.

What’s the difference between Starter and Growth?

Starter includes three pages and the essentials for a clear web presence. Growth includes up to eight pages, foundational local SEO, eligible Google Business Profile setup or optimization, service area pages, and basic Google Analytics 4 page-view tracking.

What if I need more than 8 pages?

Additional standard pages beyond the 8 included with Growth are $350 each. Pages with custom scope are quoted separately before work begins.

What exactly happens after I reach out?

You’ll answer a few questions so we can confirm fit. Then you’ll receive a clear recommendation for your situation and next steps. No pressure, no sales scripts.

How long does a website take?

Starter typically takes 2–4 weeks and Growth 3–6 weeks from kickoff. Timing can shift if content or feedback is delayed.

How many revisions are included?

Projects include structured feedback rounds to keep things moving. The estimate stage defines what’s included so revisions don’t become an open-ended loop.

Are domain and hosting fees included?

No. Domain registration and third-party hosting fees are separate. The provider, plan, and renewal costs are confirmed before launch.

Will I own the website and my content?

Yes — your content is yours. Site ownership and access are clarified during onboarding so there’s no confusion.

What’s included after launch?

Both packages include 30 days of fixes for issues with the delivered site. After that, updates are scoped and quoted before work begins, so you only pay when you request changes. Larger changes and new features are scoped separately; monthly maintenance subscriptions are not offered.
