Questions before taking the next step

These are the common “wait, but what about…?”

Why can’t you price by page count?

Because the value (and effort) comes from planning, messaging, structure, and conversion setup — not just the number of pages. The goal is results and clarity, not a bigger sitemap.

What exactly happens after I reach out?

You’ll answer a few questions so we can confirm fit. Then you’ll receive a clear recommendation for your situation and next steps. No pressure, no sales scripts.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. The setup fee is split into two payments: 50% due at the start of the project and 50% due upon completion. Monthly Website Care payments begin only after your website is completed. All terms are confirmed during the estimate stage.

How many revisions are included?

Projects include structured feedback rounds to keep things moving. The estimate stage defines what’s included so revisions don’t become an open-ended loop.

Will I own the website and my content?

Yes — your content is yours. Site ownership and access are clarified during onboarding so there’s no confusion.

How are monthly payments handled?

Monthly payments begin after your website is completed and are billed automatically via Stripe using your credit card or ACH (bank transfer). You’ll receive receipts and can manage your payment method securely through Stripe’s customer portal.
