Turning Traffic Into Inquiries for a Local Service Business

A homepage diagnosis showing why visitors weren’t becoming inquiries — and what to fix first.

View diagnosis →

Situation: Visitors were arriving from Google/referrals, but inquiries stayed low.

Scorecard flags: Value proposition clarity (2/5), Trust & proof (1/5), CTA strength (2/5).

Fix: Rewrote the hero for outcomes, consolidated to one primary CTA, and added proof + risk-reduction before the contact step.

Result: A prioritized roadmap focused on inquiry generation—not cosmetic redesign.

A “Great-Looking” Homepage That Still Didn’t Convert

A breakdown of why strong design alone wasn’t enough to earn trust or action.

View diagnosis →

Situation: The site looked premium, but inquiries were inconsistent and unpredictable.

Scorecard flags: Customer-focused messaging (2/5), Objections & risk reduction (1/5), Conversion structure (3/5).

Fix: Shifted copy from “about us” to customer outcomes, added risk-reduction near the CTA, and clarified the next step (what happens after you reach out).

Result: The homepage became a persuasion layer that earns the inquiry.

Avoiding a Full Redesign by Fixing the Real Bottleneck

How diagnosing the homepage first prevented unnecessary redesign work.

View diagnosis →

Situation: The business assumed “the site is outdated” and planned a full rebuild.

Scorecard flags: UX & mobile usability (4/5), Value proposition clarity (2/5), CTA strength (1/5).

Fix: Refocused the hero message, rebuilt CTA placement/wording, and handled common objections before the contact section.

Result: Higher ROI changes without unnecessary scope creep.
