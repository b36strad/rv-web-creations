# RV Web Creations: Customer Avatar

**Customer Avatar: “Growth-Minded Small Business Owner”**

---

**Demographics:**
- Age: 28–55
- Gender: Any
- Location: Primarily U.S., local/regional focus but open to remote work
- Business Stage: Startup, growth, or established small business

**Psychographics:**
- Values professionalism, reliability, and clear communication
- Wants a website that stands out, attracts leads, and reflects their brand personality
- Seeks transparency in pricing and process
- Appreciates creative, bold ideas and direct, friendly service
- Prefers working with a partner who “gets” their business and offers guidance

**Goals:**
- Differentiate their business online
- Convert more visitors into customers
- Build credibility and trust
- Save time and avoid tech headaches
- Get a website that’s easy to update and maintain

**Pain Points:**
- Frustrated by generic, cookie-cutter web agencies
- Overwhelmed by tech jargon and unclear processes
- Worried about hidden costs or poor communication
- Needs a site that’s both beautiful and functional

**Buying Triggers:**
- Sees bold, unique portfolio examples
- Reads clear, benefit-driven service descriptions
- Encounters playful, engaging intake and contact forms
- Finds transparent pricing and guarantees
- Feels a personal connection through storytelling and direct language

**Objections:**
- “Will this really help me stand out?”
- “Is the process simple and clear?”
- “Can I trust this agency to deliver on time and on budget?”

---

This avatar represents the ideal client your site attracts: a small business owner who wants more than just a website—they want a partner who helps them grow, stands out, and makes the process enjoyable and clear.
