INFORMATION TO GATHER BEFORE CREATING POLICIES
================================================================================

FOR PRIVACY POLICY:
-------------------
☐ Business Information
  - Legal business name
  - Business address
  - Contact email
  - Phone number (optional)
  - Website URL

☐ Data Collection
  - Contact form data (names, emails, phone numbers)
  - Project information (briefs, requirements, feedback)
  - Payment information (credit card, billing address)
  - Communication records (emails, messages)
  - Website analytics (Google Analytics, if used)
  - Cookies used on your site

☐ Data Usage
  - Project delivery and management
  - Client communication
  - Invoicing and payment processing
  - Service improvement
  - Legal compliance

☐ Data Storage & Security
  - Where data is stored (hosting provider, cloud services)
  - Security measures (SSL, encryption, access controls)
  - Data retention period (how long you keep client data)
  - Backup procedures

☐ Third-Party Services Used
  - Hosting provider (e.g., Bluehost, SiteGround, AWS)
  - Payment processor (e.g., Stripe, PayPal, Square)
  - Email service (e.g., Gmail, custom email)
  - Analytics (e.g., Google Analytics)
  - Project management tools (e.g., Trello, Asana)
  - Communication tools (e.g., Zoom, Slack)

☐ Data Sharing
  - Do you share data with third parties? (usually NO for web dev)
  - Do you sell data? (should be NO)
  - Do you use data for marketing? (specify if yes)

☐ User Rights
  - Right to access their data
  - Right to request data deletion
  - Right to correct inaccurate data
  - Right to data portability
  - How users can exercise these rights


FOR TERMS & CONDITIONS:
-----------------------
☐ Services Offered
  - Web design
  - Web development
  - Website maintenance
  - Hosting setup/management
  - Domain registration assistance
  - Other services (list specifically)

☐ Project Process
  - Discovery phase requirements
  - Content and asset requirements from client
  - Revision policy (how many rounds included)
  - Approval process
  - Timeline and milestones
  - Communication expectations

☐ Payment Terms
  - Pricing structure (flat rate, hourly, package)
  - Deposit requirements (e.g., 50% upfront)
  - Payment milestones
  - Payment methods accepted
  - Late payment policy
  - Refund policy
  - Cancellation terms

☐ Intellectual Property
  - Who owns the final website (client)
  - Who owns the code/design before full payment
  - License to use frameworks/libraries
  - Stock photo/asset licensing
  - Client's responsibility for provided content
  - Your right to showcase work in portfolio

☐ Client Responsibilities
  - Provide accurate information
  - Provide content and assets on time
  - Provide timely feedback and approvals
  - Pay invoices on schedule
  - Maintain website after handoff (if applicable)

☐ Your Responsibilities
  - Deliver agreed-upon services
  - Meet reasonable timelines
  - Communicate progress
  - Maintain confidentiality

☐ Warranties & Limitations
  - What you guarantee (e.g., "website will function as designed")
  - What you don't guarantee (e.g., "specific search engine rankings")
  - Browser/device compatibility scope
  - Testing procedures
  - Bug fix period after launch

☐ Liability Limitations
  - Maximum liability amount
  - Exclusion of indirect/consequential damages
  - Client's responsibility for their content
  - Force majeure clause

☐ Termination
  - How either party can end the agreement
  - Notice period required
  - What happens to work in progress
  - Payment obligations upon termination
  - Return of client materials

☐ Dispute Resolution
  - Governing law (your state/country)
  - Venue for legal disputes
  - Mediation/arbitration requirements
  - Attorney fees responsibility

☐ General Terms
  - How terms can be modified
  - Entire agreement clause
  - Severability
  - Assignment of agreement

================================================================================
IMPORTANT LEGAL CONSIDERATIONS FOR WEB DEVELOPMENT BUSINESS
================================================================================

1. CLIENT CONTRACTS
   - Always use written contracts for projects
   - Include scope, timeline, payment terms, IP ownership
   - Have clients sign before starting work
   - Consider separate Service Agreement vs website T&Cs

2. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
   - Clarify who owns code, designs, content
   - Typically: Client owns final product after full payment
   - You retain right to reuse general techniques/frameworks
   - Get written permission to use project in portfolio

3. DATA PROTECTION
   - Use SSL certificates on all client websites
   - Secure client data you collect
   - Don't share client information without permission
   - Have data breach notification plan

4. LIABILITY PROTECTION
   - Consider business insurance (errors & omissions)
   - Use contracts to limit liability
   - Set realistic expectations with clients
   - Document all client communications

5. TAXES & BUSINESS STRUCTURE
   - Ensure proper business registration
   - Keep separate business/personal finances
   - Track expenses for tax purposes
   - Consider LLC for liability protection
