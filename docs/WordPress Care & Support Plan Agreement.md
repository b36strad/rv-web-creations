### Software Updates

These are tasks that are done throughout the month. Some items, such as updates, only occur when necessary and when we know the update is a stable version and it is okay to proceed.

Each week, at our discretion, we will apply available updates for the WordPress core software, theme files, and plugin files. We also watch WordPress industry security news and proactively perform these updates more frequently during times of enhanced threat levels.

While it is impossible to guarantee that your site will never be hacked, all of these techniques make your website a more difficult target for hackers. In addition, it is well known that hackers tend to exploit easier targets first – sites without security measures in place. 

However, in the event that your site is hacked, we will restore it to pre-hack condition at no additional cost either by restoring a site backup or performing a malware cleanup at our discretion.

### Theme and Plugin Licenses and Updates

One of the most important strengths of WordPress is its immense ecosystem of third-party add-on software called themes and plugins. Some of these require no specific licensing for use on your website and are free for anyone to use.

However, we use a premium WordPress theme/builder and several premium WordPress plugins that require an annual licensing fee for ongoing updates and support. Premium themes and plugins used on your website are provided at their current software version for as long as you remain with one of our care & support plans. Future updates and security patches for premium themes and plugins are covered.

If your website uses premium themes and/or plugins and we do not have appropriate licensing that we used specifically for your website, the responsibility is yours for maintaining the appropriate licensing to ensure the availability of updates.

### Technical Support Scope

JoLi's support is limited to website-related issues only. We do not provide technical support for email problems, computer troubleshooting, third-party software, or any issues unrelated to your website. Please contact the relevant service provider for non-website technical assistance.

### Third Party Services

From time to time, our clients might employ a third party (such as, but not limited to, a Search Engine Optimization professional, social media professional, or content writer) whose services involve modifications to the website.

Please notify us if someone else needs to do additional work for you and we will add them as an appropriate user on your website. This keeps our hosting server secure as well as your own website and other client websites that reside on our server. This is done to prevent potential complications that could arise from unregulated changes. Editing the website's code and design without a deep understanding of its structure can result in unexpected behavior or even downtime.

We cannot be responsible for the work of a 3rd party service. Should our services be necessary due to any work performed by a third party, these services are billable at our current hourly rate.

If payment is more than 7 days late without communication, your website will be placed in Maintenance Mode, making it inaccessible until payment is made in full for the overdue amount and a $100 fee to restore your website in full.

You may cancel your plan at any time with a 30-day notice. In the event of any termination of this agreement by client, no refunds shall be given under any circumstances. If you choose to discontinue your WordPress Care & Support Plan, the total responsibility is yours for performing backups and keeping all components of your website secure and updated.

Upon termination of your WordPress Care & Support Plan, we will provide a backup created using an industry standard WordPress backup system and will provide a WordPress Maintenance Guide that will include necessary licenses you will need to purchase and a checklist of tasks you will be responsible for.
