## Introduction

This service agreement template is designed for web design and development projects undertaken by RV Web Creations LLC. It establishes clear expectations, protects both parties, and ensures smooth project execution.

**Purpose:**
- Define project scope and deliverables
- Establish payment terms and schedules
- Clarify ownership and intellectual property rights
- Set timelines and revision policies
- Outline responsibilities of both parties

## Termination

### Termination by Client
Client may terminate agreement by providing [14] days written notice.

**If terminated before completion:**
- Client pays for work completed to date
- Client receives files and work completed at time of termination
- Deposit is non-refundable
- Any remaining balance calculated based on percentage complete

### Termination by Provider
Provider may terminate if:
- Client fails to pay invoices within [30] days
- Client fails to provide necessary materials after [2] written requests
- Client breaches terms of this agreement
- Relationship becomes untenable or unworkable

**Upon provider termination:**
- Client receives all completed work to date
- Payment required for work completed
- Refund of unearned deposits (pro-rated)

### Effect of Termination
Upon termination:
- All work stops immediately
- Outstanding invoices become due immediately
- Intellectual property rights transfer per payment status
- Each party returns confidential materials
- Website access and hosting transfer arranged

## General Provisions

### Entire Agreement
- This agreement constitutes the entire agreement between parties
- Supersedes all prior negotiations, agreements, and understandings
- Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties

### Force Majeure
Neither party liable for delays due to circumstances beyond reasonable control:
- Natural disasters
- Acts of government or war
- Internet or hosting service failures
- Pandemics or public health emergencies
- Other unforeseen circumstances

### Independent Contractor
- Provider is independent contractor, not employee
- Provider responsible for own taxes and insurance
- No employment relationship created

### Assignment
- Client may not assign agreement without provider's written consent
- Provider may subcontract work as needed while maintaining responsibility

### Severability
- If any provision is found invalid, remainder of agreement remains in effect
- Invalid provision replaced with valid provision closest to original intent

### Waiver
- Failure to enforce any provision does not waive right to enforce later
- Waiver must be in writing to be effective

### Notices
All notices must be in writing and delivered to:
- Email addresses listed in this agreement
- Certified mail to addresses listed in this agreement
- Notices effective upon receipt

### Counterparts and Electronic Signatures
- Agreement may be signed in counterparts
- Electronic signatures legally binding
- Scanned or digital copies acceptable
