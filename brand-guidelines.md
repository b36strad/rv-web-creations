# RV Web Creations — Brand Guidelines

## Brand Overview
RV Web Creations delivers custom business websites with a focus on clarity, professionalism, and results. Our brand identity is modern, trustworthy, and approachable, using a distinctive navy and gold palette.

---

## Logo Usage
- Use the official SVG logo with navy background (#1f4f7b), gold (#f8b400), and white text.
- Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "R" in the logo.
- Do not stretch, recolor, or alter the logo.

---

## Color Palette
| Color Name      | Hex       | Usage                        |

| Primary        | #1f4f7b   | Main brand/navy, buttons     |

---

## Typography
- **Font Family:**
  - system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif
- **Body Font Size:** 17px
- **Headings:** Bold, often centered, navy or gold accent
- **Lead Text:** #2d3748, 1.15rem
- **Small Text:** 0.875rem

---

## Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Navy background (#1f4f7b), white text
- **Gold CTA Button:** Gold background (#f8b400), dark text
- **Button Hover:** Lighter navy (#2f6aa3) or darker gold (#e6a500)

---

## Visual Elements
- **Cards/Sections:** Rounded corners (1.5rem), subtle shadows
- **Badges:** Pill-shaped, navy or gold, no wrap
- **Borders:** Left border accent for callouts (#dee2e6 or brand colors)
- **Icons:** Use Bootstrap Icons or simple SVGs in brand colors

---

## Imagery
- Use high-quality, business-relevant images with a clean, modern aesthetic
- Prefer images with blue, gold, or neutral tones
- Avoid cluttered or overly busy visuals

---

## Voice & Tone
- Clear, direct, and professional
- Friendly and approachable, but never casual or slangy
- Focus on client results, clarity, and trust

---

## Usage Examples
- **Website:** Consistent use of navy and gold, bold headings, clear CTAs
- **Social Media:** Use logo, brand colors, and fonts in graphics
- **Documents:** Proposals, invoices, and presentations should use brand fonts, colors, and logo
- **Email:** Signature with logo, navy/gold accents
